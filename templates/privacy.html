Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create an account, we collect your name and email address. When you use the app, we store the expense records you enter — including amounts, categories, dates, and any descriptions you provide. We do not collect payment information or government-issued identification.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to operate Spendly — to display your expenses, generate summaries, and let you manage your records. We do not use your financial data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond providing the service to you.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored on secure servers. We retain your account data for as long as your account remains active. If you delete your account, your data is permanently removed. We take reasonable technical measures to protect your data against unauthorised access.

4. Third-Party Services

Spendly uses Google Fonts for typography, loaded directly from Google's servers. No expense data or personally identifiable information is shared with any third-party service. We do not use analytics trackers, advertising networks, or data brokers.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about how your data is handled, please reach out through the app's support channel. We will respond within a reasonable time.
